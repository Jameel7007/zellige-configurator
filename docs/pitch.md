# Pitch

A draft to send to tile makers and tile shops. Nothing here has been sent. The parts in
[square brackets] need filling in first.

## Who to send it to

- Workshops and shops that sell made-to-order zellige or mosaic and quote by email.
- Tile studios whose site shows a gallery and a "contact us for a quote" form, and no way to
  try colours.
- Interior designers who specify patterned tile and redraw it for each client.

A good sign: their site says "custom colours available" and shows a contact form.

## The email

> **Subject:** Let your customers design their own panel
>
> Hello [name],
>
> I build 3D tools for the web. I made a demo that I think fits [workshop]: a page where a
> customer designs a zellige panel and you receive the tile count.
>
> https://jameel7007.github.io/zellige-configurator/
>
> They choose the centrepiece, the size and the glazes, see it on a wall or in a floor,
> and send you a quote request. You get the number of pieces per glaze, the tiles to
> buy, a one-page spec sheet and a cut plan at real size.
>
> The demo uses my own pattern and sample prices. For you it would use your patterns, your
> glazes and your price list.
>
> Would a 15-minute call next week be useful? I can show it with one of your designs.
>
> Thomas Mooney
> [phone or site]

Keep it this short. The demo does the selling; the email only has to get it opened.

## What to offer

| Stage | What they get | Rough time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Fit | Their logo, colours and glaze range; their price table; hosted on their domain | 1 to 2 weeks |
| 2. Their patterns | One to three of their own designs built into the engine | 1 to 2 weeks per pattern |
| 3. Their workflow | Quote requests into their inbox or CRM, cut plans tested on their cutting software | by agreement |

Price stage 1 as a fixed fee. It is small on purpose: it lets a maker say yes without a
meeting about budget, and it puts a live tool on their site that their customers use.

## Questions they will ask

**"Are the tile counts right?"** The counts are exact for the pattern: every piece is a real
shape and tests check that the pieces fill the panel with no gaps. How many tiles to buy
depends on their tile size and how much they lose in cutting. Those are two numbers they
give me.

**"Can it do our patterns?"** Patterns built from stars and rosettes on a grid, yes. Each
new one is real work (stage 2), not a setting. A freehand or pictorial design is a different
tool.

**"Does it work on a phone?"** Yes. [Check it on a real phone before saying so.]

**"Can our cutter use the file?"** It writes DXF, the common format. It has not been tried
on a machine yet, so the honest answer is "we test it on yours in stage 3".

**"What does it cost to run?"** Nothing beyond their existing hosting. It is static files
with no server and no customer database.

## Before sending

1. Open the live demo on a real phone.
2. Open a DXF in a CAD program (LibreCAD is free) and confirm the pieces and layers.
3. Decide the stage 1 fee.
